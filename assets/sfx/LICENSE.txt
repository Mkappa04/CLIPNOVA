CLIPNOVA built-in sound effects: whoosh, impact, bass_drop, riser, explosion, hit, transition, ui,
cinematic, gaming, pop, ding.

These files are synthesised from scratch by backend/editing/effects.py (generate_builtin):
pure tones and seeded noise shaped by envelopes, no third-party recordings or samples.
They are dedicated to the public domain (CC0 1.0): use them anywhere, including commercially.

Your own sounds: import them from the "Libreria audio" page (license and source required),
or put files you hold the rights to in data/sfx/<category>/. They take precedence over the built-in ones.
