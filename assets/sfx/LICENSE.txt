CLIPNOVA built-in sound effects (whoosh, impact, pop, riser, ding).

These files are synthesised from scratch by backend/editing/effects.py (generate_builtin):
pure tones and seeded noise shaped by envelopes, no third-party recordings or samples.
They are dedicated to the public domain (CC0 1.0): use them anywhere, including commercially.

To use your own sounds put files you hold the rights to in data/sfx/<category>/
(category = whoosh | impact | pop | riser | ding); they replace the built-in ones.
